# Publication standards and disclosures

Last reviewed: **30 July 2026**

This page states what this publication is, what was checked, and what readers
should not infer from those checks.

## Educational scope

This is a static educational ebook for professional learning. It is not:

- medical advice, emergency guidance, or patient-specific clinical decision
  support;
- a diagnostic or treatment recommendation;
- a validated medical device, regulatory clearance, or authorization to deploy
  a model;
- local clinical, research, privacy, security, or institutional policy; or
- an endorsement by an employer, health system, university, regulator,
  standards body, vendor, journal, or cited author.

Readers must verify consequential claims against current primary sources,
applicable law and regulation, and local policy. Patient care and operational
decisions require qualified human judgment and the responsible local process.

## Authorship and AI assistance

AI tools have assisted with drafting, editing, code, testing, and review during
development. The maintainer selected the scope and structure, revised the
material, decided what to publish, and remains responsible for the release.
AI-generated output is not treated as a source or authority. Factual claims
should be supported by the cited primary or official source, not by an AI
system's wording.

This disclosure does not assert that every passage is independently
copyrightable. The license grants only rights the publisher actually controls.

  the publisher controls the relevant rights; and
- site software and configuration are offered under the ISC License.

Third-party components, trademarks, cited publications, facts, public-domain
material, and material outside the publisher's control are excluded from the
book license. The complete terms and notices are the `LICENSE` and
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` files in the
[source repository](https://github.com/rkalani1/ML/).

Current teaching figures are hash-inventoried and linked to repository history
in the asset rights register. That traceability supports review but does not
prove independent creation or eliminate all similarity and ownership risk.
Theme controls use Pictogrammers Material Design icons under Apache 2.0, and
the repository link uses a Font Awesome GitHub brand icon under CC BY 4.0.
Those names and marks identify their respective projects and do not imply
endorsement.

## Evidence and currency

The [evidence register](evidence-register.md) is a bounded log of selected
high-consequence claims checked against primary or official sources. A listed
row applies only to the claim, population, endpoint, and date recorded there.
It is not a claim that every sentence has been peer reviewed, that a benchmark
remains current, or that a model is clinically useful.

Worked numerical examples are synthetic unless a specific study or dataset is
named. Synthetic values teach a method; they are not treatment thresholds,
deployment specifications, expected performance, or promises about outcomes.

## Privacy and data handling

The published site has no user accounts, uploads, or data-submission forms.
Theme search and palette controls run locally in the browser and use form
markup without submitting reader data. The site has no advertising, configured
analytics, or cookies set by the publisher. It does not ask for or intentionally
collect patient data. Do not submit clinical cases, identifiers, protected
health information, confidential institutional data, or credentials through
the repository.

Pages containing mathematical notation load MathJax and its versioned math-font
files from jsDelivr over HTTPS. Those requests can disclose ordinary connection
metadata such as IP address and user agent to the delivery provider. GitHub
Pages and network intermediaries may also process ordinary web-server logs
under their own terms. Other site assets are served with the publication. The
deployed copy of the
[Apache License 2.0](assets/licenses/Apache-2.0.txt) accompanies applicable
MathJax and theme-bundled icon notices.

## Accessibility

The release process checks semantic structure, alternative text, link and asset
integrity, color-scheme support, responsive layouts, and rendered output.
Keyboard navigation, focus visibility, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, and screen
reader behavior are also reviewed. The engineering target is WCAG 2.2 AA;
conformance is not claimed solely from automated testing.

If a page is difficult to use, please report the URL, browser or assistive
technology, and the barrier without including private information.

## Corrections, rights concerns, and security

For factual, accessibility, attribution, or rights concerns that are safe to
discuss publicly, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/rkalani1/ML/issues). Identify the exact page
or asset and the requested correction. Do not post patient data, confidential
material, or unnecessary personal information.

Report suspected vulnerabilities through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/rkalani1/ML/security/advisories/new)
as described in the repository's `SECURITY.md`. Substantiated problems may be
corrected, attributed, replaced, or removed.

## Limits of the review

Automated checks cannot establish legal non-infringement, clinical validity,
regulatory status, or universal accessibility. The 30 July 2026 review covered
the current release-candidate source tree and locally rendered output, present
dependencies and assets, selected claims, and documented repository
provenance. It did not constitute a legal opinion or
independent specialist peer review; it did not determine the effect of any
employment agreement, institutional intellectual-property policy, prior
publication agreement, or contributor assignment; and it did not prove that
every historical Git object is free of sensitive or third-party material.
