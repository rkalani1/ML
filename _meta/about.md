# About, licensing, and provenance

## Mission

Provide a free, updatable machine-learning and AI curriculum centered on
neurology and stroke, with explicit evidence, rights, privacy, and release
boundaries.

## Originality bar (hard rules)

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Publisher-controlled teaching prose | Substantial similarity to commercial textbook prose |
| Synthetic clinical vignettes | Real patient stories / PHI |
| Repository-provenanced teaching figures | Lifted paper or book figures |
| Publisher-controlled teaching tables | Guideline instrument dumps; paper result tables |
| Named standards + links | Pasted full copyrighted checklists/instruments |
| Primary-source citations | Paper abstracts pasted as content |
| Synthetic worked numbers | Publisher PDF text |

This Web Edition was adapted from an internal curriculum project. Repository
history, exact asset hashes, and current-tree scans support provenance review.
They do not independently prove authorship, copyrightability, chain of title,
or absence of all substantial similarity. AI tools assisted development; the
maintainer selected, revised, reviewed, and decided what to publish.

## Accuracy stance

- Prediction ≠ causation unless the design supports causal claims  
- Prefer calibration, external validation, and decision impact over AUROC theater  
- Recompute worked examples; do not trust marketing demos  
- Data leakage, site shift, and label noise matter as much as architecture novelty
